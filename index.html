Dashain Festival

Presented by: Adip Uchai Thakuri

Welcome! This is a simple presentation about Dashain, the biggest and most important festival in Nepal.

Slide 2 of 6

What is Dashain?

  • Dashain is the biggest festival in Nepal.
  • It celebrates the victory of good over evil.
  • Families come together to celebrate, eat good food, and receive blessings.
Slide 3 of 6

Tika and Jamara

  • Elders put Tika (red color paste) on our foreheads.
  • They give us Jamara (sacred yellow grass).
  • They give us blessings for a good and happy life.
Slide 4 of 6

Bamboo Swing (Linge Ping)

  • People make big bamboo swings called Linge Ping.
  • It is fun to play on the swing during Dashain.
  • It is believed that everyone must leave the ground at least once during Dashain.
Slide 5 of 6

Special Food: Sel Roti

  • Sel Roti is a traditional sweet ring-shaped bread made of rice flour.
  • Families cook it together during Dashain.
  • It is very tasty and popular during the festival.
Slide 6 of 6

Conclusion

  • Dashain brings families and friends together.
  • It fills our hearts with joy, love, and peace.
  • Thank you for listening! Happy Dashain!
